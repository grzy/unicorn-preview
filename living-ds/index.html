Unicorn · Living Design System

A creative OS for designers
who work in the terminal.

Unicorn sits next to Claude Code. It tracks your quests and experiments; its Story panel keeps the taste, the Figma canvas and the weekly story behind every build. This page holds its colors, type, corners and components, token for token with the app.

01The mark
The unicorn is built from terminal block glyphs, not an illustration. The exact same pixel sprite, Peach, is the single logo across every surface: the terminal hook that prints at the start of each Claude Code session, the desktop app icon, the preloader, and the Logo row in Taste. It is rendered as text or solid pixels, never as a smooth vector, so it always reads as made of pixels.

The pixel unicorn, Peach. A dark horn, a lilac mane and tail, a peach body, darker-peach legs. Born in the terminal banner, it carries through every surface as the one consistent character. Hand-made, never scraped, never smoothed.
The desktop app icon. The same pixel unicorn, Peach, standing on a fully transparent icon: no squircle, no stroke, just the pixels filling the frame on Apple's icon grid. The mark is the icon.
The family. Every unicorn is named after a color in the design system. Same size, same ground line, each a little different.
The agent. Peach shows what your agents are doing, in the Quest panel and on the roaming layer. The rare state is Lilac’s visit.
02Core palette
Two signature colors carry everything: peach and lilac. Daikon is the text; Nori Dark, the near-black, is the ground, and Nori is the softer dark. Tomato is a rare accent for destructive or urgent moments only; Watermelon is its calmer twin, the delete × at rest, deepening to tomato on hover.

03The signature move · two gradient strokes
Peach to lilac is the one gesture that signals Unicorn, and it shows up as a 1px gradient stroke in two forms, matched to scale. Both are drawn with the same mask-composite technique so the gradient sits on the edge, not the fill -- light does the work, fills are avoided.
Even stroke
① Even -- peach→lilac runs the full perimeter at even weight, no corner fade. On the small UI: buttons, chips, labels, tabs, filters.
Faded corners
② Faded corners -- the gradient runs bottom-left→top-right and whispers off to transparent at the top-right + bottom-left tips. On the large chrome: panels, the window's glass edge and the preloader.
Gradient text
Where each form lands. The signature shows up two ways depending on the surface -- and the banners break from both: they are filled, not stroked.
Preloader card · window edge
Preloader card + the app window edge -- the faded-corner gradient stroke over the glass / Nori Dark, same edge language as the panels. The peach→lilac rides the stroke here, never a fill.

06Glass & geometry
On the desktop the glass is painted, not sampled: a near-opaque base (rgba(21,20,25,0.99)) under two crossing gradients -- a soft top-left light sheen and a faint lilac→peach wash from the bottom-right -- so panels stay legible over a busy terminal. The web preview uses the translucent fill (rgba(22,22,22,0.78)) with a 26px backdrop blur. Both carry a soft drop shadow (0 6px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.35)) and the signature peach→lilac stroke that fades at the top-right and bottom-left corners. Only two lines survive inside a panel: the 2px peach→lilac hairline under each title (it ends on lilac, no fade) and the quest progress fill. Recessed wells (the Currently card, the archived pill, Completed chips) sink with rgba(0,0,0,0.18), never a white wash. Radii: 18px for panels, 6px for chips and inputs.

07Buttons & chips
Agent strip + minimized Quest: minimized, the Quest panel is titled with the quest's own name. Line 1 is the status (Working / Waiting for You / No agent running); line 2 is what it's on (the started experiment, else the quest) or, while waiting, what it needs (Your reply, Permission: Run a command); on hover line 2 becomes Open Session / Open Terminal. Commits: Fewer · More left, the count centered, Sound On / Off right, all 11px (Sound medium). Month pager: carets at rest; while the panel is hovered (when the min/max caret appears) each caret grows into an arrow -- the head slides out and the line draws in behind it, one solid stroke so nothing overlaps. Faint at rest, bright on hover, never a fill.
Chips (Paper · Design · Week, Quest · Story) are one size app-wide: Ubuntu Sans Medium 12px, 6px × 8px padding, 6px corners. The selected chip is the filled gradient with a Nori Dark label and does not change on hover; the others lift to a soft gray. Quiet links (Directory, Show in Finder) share the 12px size, mute at rest and Daikon on hover; Show in Finder appears only on hover, and mid-drop the same spot shows the destination path in lilac.

08Experiment status
Each experiment row is [dot] name ... ×: the status sits hard left in a fixed slot, the × in a reserved slot on the right, so nothing moves under the cursor. Click the dot for a small menu: Started, Paused, Finished, Delete Experiment. Color carries the signal: peach means an agent is on it now (the row says Now in peach), lilac is started, a gray pause is set aside (it doesn't count toward active work), and a gray check is finished. 09The Week page
Story › Week opens Builder's Week: one page per quest per week (Sunday to Saturday), composed fresh from real data only, the quest's experiments, this week's commits on that quest, its keeps and files. Cards: Still true?, Agents right now, Experiments, Files, Keep, To-do and the week's commits. Past weeks live in the quest's Directory. Self-contained HTML in ~/unicorn/briefs/<quest>/.
10Motion principles
  • No AI lift. Photos and cards don't scale or brighten on hover. Static is the goal; the lift is the tell.
  • Delicate arrival, decisive exit. Hover effects ease in slower than they ease out, so nothing lingers.
  • Ramp both ways. Nothing pops in or cuts out. Everything fades, with generous easing.
  • Depth from light + edge. Surface comes from gradient, blur, and glass; the only line is the signature peach→lilac gradient stroke. No flat outlines.
  • The macOS grow-out. The preview terminal genie-opens from its bottom-center -- a small scale with a touch of back-ease overshoot -- so it reads like a real window waking up, then holds open and auto-types.
  • Respect reduced-motion. Every animation gates behind the OS preference.
11Sound · a first-class material
Sound is designed, not decorated. A single warm steel-pan voice (Tone.js AMSynth, a sine through chorus + reverb) speaks for the whole app, so every cue feels like one instrument in one room. Each gesture earns its own phrase -- never a generic system beep.
The voice
Warm steel-pan AMSynth, sine oscillator, pulled-in envelope so a tap blooms and rings briefly instead of sustaining. The same voice that plays the Commits calendar's ambient music powers the UI.
The phrases
A soft tap on clicks. A launch bloom -- a slow ascending pentatonic run -- as the preloader wakes. A celebratory ascending arpeggio flourish when this week's page is composed (Week, then Open). Rising / falling single notes on panel float in / out. A victory run on closing a loop. And the steel-pan soundtrack under the commit calendar.
Every cue only starts after a user gesture (browsers block autoplay), so the room stays quiet until you act. After 20 seconds of silence the audio engine sleeps, and the next note wakes it.
12Desktop treatment
In the Electron app the window is transparent: panels float on your real desktop, beside the terminal. HUD mode keeps Unicorn on top and lets clicks pass through the gaps to the terminal underneath, so Claude Code and Unicorn are usable at once. The preloader, panels, and greeting all soften so the desktop stays visible. Unicorn draws its own traffic lights in tomato / peach / lilac, wearing the panels' peach→lilac ring as their edge. The menubar carries one control, Panels: a popover that shows or hides Quest · Story · Keep · Commits · Activity · Settings (⌘1-5 / ⌘,) and holds Roaming Unicorns and Float on Top (⌥U). Hold ⌘ and drag to move the window.
13Voice · git-ty up
The rally cry is "git-ty up", a git pun on the cowboy's call, and the one phrase that carries Unicorn's personality. It prints beside the mark in the terminal hook: ✺ Unicorn git-ty up, no dot. It is terminal output, so it is drawn as terminal text, never as a button. Used sparingly, as a signature delight, never a slogan plastered everywhere. Voice notes that ride alongside it: Ships at midnight Pacific.
