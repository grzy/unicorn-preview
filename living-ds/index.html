Title Case

  • Titles, buttons, menus and counts: Complete This Quest, Hold ⌘ and Drag, 27 Finished.
  • Small words stay lowercase: a, an, and, as, at, by, for, in, of, on, or, the, to.
  • Tooltips that are phrases use sentence case: Open this build’s folder.
  • “&” is a house signature: Backup & Restore.

Never

  • ALL CAPS LABELS Labels are words, in Title Case.
  • Mute Sound On and Sound Off.
  • Day 140 Weeks are calendar weeks: Week 42.
  • Em dashes, process notes in the UI, symbols on section labels.

Corners

Three radii, one per size of thing. Panels are 18, the boxes inside them 10, buttons and chips 6. Nothing is a pill.

Buttons

One filled button, one quiet one. The fill is the action that needs you; the quiet one is text, no box. Hover is a touch brighter and stays filled, eased in and out. No strokes, no lift.
